# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user wears an M5Stack StopWatch as a glanceable companion to local Codex and Assistant workflows. Interactions are brief, often one-handed, and must remain legible and predictable on a 466-pixel square display.

## Product Purpose

StopWatch turns the device into a launcher, Codex status monitor, Assistant, and compact utility surface. Success means important state is understandable at a glance and common actions complete without taking attention away from the user's main computer.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, technical, immediate.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative motion, web-dashboard ornament, delayed choreography, flashing rebuilds, bouncy transitions, and unfamiliar controls that make a small embedded interface feel less direct.

## Design Principles

- Make state changes spatially clear and immediately responsive.
- Deliver critical workflow state through an event-driven fast path, with
  bounded polling only as a recovery mechanism.
- Preserve familiar controls and consistent gesture ownership.
- Spend the limited motion budget only on feedback and navigation.
- Prefer bounded, hardware-efficient behavior over decorative effects.
- Keep critical status readable without relying on color or motion alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, generous touch targets, and short non-bouncing transitions. Motion must never hide information or be required to understand state; every animated transition must also have a valid immediate final state.
